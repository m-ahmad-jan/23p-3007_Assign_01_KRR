Saba Hashmi was born in Gujranwala in 1935. 
She studied at Calder College of Science and later at Northgate Institute of Physics. 
She received the Riverbend Gold Medal in 1980. 
Colleagues usually referred to her simply as "Dr. Hashmi". 
In 1966, Dr. Hashmi joined Westmere College of Science. 
Saba Hashmi's doctoral advisor was Waleed Tarar. 
He later moved to Thornfield College of Science. 
Despite a persistent rumour, she never studied at Northgate College of Science.
